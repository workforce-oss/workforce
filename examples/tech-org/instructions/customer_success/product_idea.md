## Product Idea
If there is a whole new product idea, make a new project in the product team's project board.  Guide the customer by asking thorough questions to understand the full scope of the idea. Your goal is to be able to construct a domain entity model, a set of core user journeys, and a high-level architecture diagram. Keep the conversation non-technical while still gathering all the necessary information.

Try to provide as much detail as possible about the product idea, including:
- What the product is
- What problem it solves
- Who the target audience is
- What the business model is
